## Workflow and access decisions

Approved after Phase 1 review. Paths are relative to `src/domain/`.

### D10 · Withdraw a submission

A Head Nurse may withdraw a submitted schedule before the Supervisor acts on it:
`SUBMITTED → FINALIZED` via `WITHDRAW`. Once the Supervisor has approved or returned it,
withdrawal no longer applies. Withdrawing a revision's submission returns it to `REVISING`
so D14's revision scope still holds.
Enforced in `schedule/state-machine.ts`.

### D11 · Nurse visibility before finalization

Before `FINALIZED`, nurses see only their own preferences and the schedule status; working
assignments stay hidden. From `FINALIZED` on they see the working schedule marked as pending
approval, until an approved version exists; from then on they see the approved version.
Enforced in `schedule/visibility.ts`.

### D12 · Supervisor visibility

A Supervisor may view the department schedule read-only from `FINALIZED` on. Approve and
return are available only in `SUBMITTED`; `FINALIZED` does not mean submitted.
Enforced in `authz/policies.ts` (`schedule.viewDepartment`) and `schedule/state-machine.ts`
(`APPROVE`/`RETURN` exist only from `SUBMITTED`).

### D13 · Change-request timing

A nurse may submit a shift-change request from `FINALIZED` on: `FINALIZED`, `SUBMITTED`,
`RETURNED`, `APPROVED`, `REVISING`. Requests never change assignments automatically.
Enforced in `authz/policies.ts` (`changeRequest.submit`).

### D14 · Revision scope after approval

In a revision of an approved schedule, the Head Nurse may edit only the explicitly opened
revision dates; all other dates stay locked. More dates require explicitly extending the
revision scope; the whole month is never unlocked implicitly.
Enforced in `schedule/assignment-editing.ts`.

### D15 · One assignment per nurse per day

Each nurse has at most one assignment per calendar day: `M`, `E`, `N` or `ME`. `ME` is the
long Morning + Evening shift and is that day's single assignment; M and E are never stored as
two assignments on the same day. A duplicate is a blocking violation.
Enforced in `rules/validate-schedule.ts` (and by the primary key in Phase 2).

### D16 · Historical access after leaving a department

A nurse whose membership has ended:

- keeps read-only access to their own historical change requests from that department;
- keeps access to their own shifts in schedules they were rostered on (subject to D11);
- cannot create new requests or edit/withdraw old ones for that department;
- gains no department-wide access.

This is historical access, not membership. A deactivated account loses all access.
Enforced in `authz/policies.ts` (`changeRequest.view`, `changeRequest.withdraw`,
`schedule.viewOwn` with the resource's `onRoster`).
